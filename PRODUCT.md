# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coached sales and real estate agents (Harry's clients, later other paying agents) who log their daily activity numbers and a short end-of-day voice reflection. Usage is mostly unhurried: a quiet moment at a desk or winding down in the evening, not a rugged field tool used mid-task outdoors. Secondary user: Harry himself in "coach mode," scanning a roster of his agents' pace and reading their reflections instead of chasing them over WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Replace Harry's current WhatsApp-plus-spreadsheet coaching workflow with a single app: agents log numbers and one voice note a day, the app turns that into automatic reporting and coach-toned, data-driven suggestions, and Harry gets visibility without manually chasing anyone. Success looks like an agent trusting the app enough to log every day without being nagged, and Harry getting a genuinely useful weekly read on each person without doing the aggregation himself.

## Brand Personality

Personal and coach-like. The app should feel like Harry is behind the screen: firm, warm, expert, invested in the person's result — not a corporate SaaS dashboard. Premium through restraint: quiet confidence, editorial typographic hierarchy, and a tightly limited navy-and-gold identity rather than decoration or UI chrome.

## Anti-references

Generic "AI-generated / vibe-coded" SaaS look, specifically: default sans stacks (Inter, Roboto, Arial), rounded-2xl/3xl radius on every surface, glowing borders, gradient text, hero-metric templates, identical icon-plus-heading card grids, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section markers, emoji used as icons, and perfectly symmetrical "Frankenstein" block layouts with no organic variation in spacing or alignment.

## Design Principles

1. **Feels coach-made, not vendor-made.** Every screen should read as built specifically for Harry's method (the Goal/Proof/Steps framework, the three-question voice note), never assembled from generic dashboard components.
2. **Restraint over decoration.** Earn "premium" through typography, spacing, and material (weight contrast, soft realistic shadow) — not through gradients, glow, or a wide color palette.
3. **Navy and gold are the identity; the muted earthy third tone is the workhorse.** Reserve the navy/gold pair for what matters (brand marks, hit targets, key actions); let the everyday surface run on the quieter tone.
4. **Calm end-of-day tool, not a field instrument.** Since usage is mostly unhurried desk/evening time, favor considered typographic hierarchy and finer detail over rugged high-contrast slabs and oversized tap targets.
5. **One coach, many agents, no admin friction.** The UI must scale cleanly from a single agent's private daily tracker to a coach's multi-client roster view without feeling like two bolted-together products.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast minimum throughout, even with a muted/earthy palette — body text and placeholder text must hit ≥4.5:1; don't let "muted" collapse into washed-out gray-on-tint. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all transitions. Standard mobile tap-target sizing, since the app is still used on a phone even in calm settings.
