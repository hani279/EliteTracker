ELITE Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 August 2026  ·  Last updated: 5 August 2026

Before you publish this: this draft is written specifically around what ELITE Tracker's code actually collects and how it actually shares data between coaches and consultants — it is not generic boilerplate. It is also not legal advice. Have a lawyer confirm it before real users see it, particularly the EU/Australia rights section and the data retention period in Section 6, which is still a placeholder for you to set.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Bycaptur ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information through ELITE Tracker (the "App"), a PWA for coach-led sales and real estate activity tracking. It applies to two kinds of users:

  • Consultants — individuals who log daily activity, pipeline, and CRM data, and are optionally linked to a coach.
  • Coaches — individuals who monitor a roster of linked consultants, send reports and nudges, and keep private notes.

By creating an account, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your name and email address. You can create an account with a password, or sign in with Google — in that case Google shares your name and email with us, subject to Google's own privacy policy. We never see or store your Google password.

Profile and coaching-relationship data

Depending on your role, we store: your consultant vertical (real estate or general sales), your daily activity targets, your recurring focus tasks, your personal goal framework, and — for coaches — a business/brand name and a shareable coach code. If you're a consultant linked to a coach, we store that link (and the coach can see it too).

Activity and performance data

The core of the App: the daily numbers you log (calls, conversations, appraisals/meetings, etc.), your daily focus checklist, logged outcomes (e.g. listings won), and any written daily reflection ("what did you do / learn / struggle with").

Voice notes

If you record a voice note, we store the audio file, and, where the feature is enabled, a text transcript and an AI-generated summary of it.

Pipeline and CRM data — including other people's information

Consultants can log prospects and deals in the App's pipeline and CRM: names, phone numbers, email addresses, deal stage, notes, and estimated value. This is personal information about your own clients and leads, not about you. By entering it, you confirm you have the right to store and process it for your own business purposes, and you are responsible for it under any privacy law that applies to you (e.g. telling your own clients how you use their data). We process it only to display it back to you and, where applicable, your linked coach.

Coach–consultant communications

Coaches can send daily report messages and nudges to their linked consultants — these are visible to both people. Coaches can also keep private notes about a consultant; those notes are visible only to the coach who wrote them, never to the consultant or any other coach.

Device and notification data

If you enable push notifications, we store a browser-issued push subscription (an endpoint and encryption keys) so we can deliver nudges and reminders. This identifies your browser/device, not you personally.

What we don't collect

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services. We don't sell your data. We don't access your device's contacts, location, camera, or photo library.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App's core functionality — tracking your activity, computing pace/targets, and generating reports.
  • To let a coach and their linked consultants see the shared data the App is designed to share between them (see Section 4).
  • To send reminders, nudges, and (if enabled) push notifications.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service, including preventing abuse of the coach-linking system.
  • To respond to you if you contact us for support.

4. How information is shared — the coach/consultant model

The App is built around a specific, limited sharing relationship, enforced at the database level (row-level security), not just in the app's interface:

If you're linked as...A coach can seeA consultant can see
Consultant → CoachYour activity numbers, pace, pipeline, goals, and reports you send themTheir name, brand, and any reports/nudges they send you
Coach's private notes about you—Never visible to you
Other consultants on the same coach's rosterNot shared with each otherNot shared with each other

We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share information:

  • With service providers who help us run the App (see Section 5) under contracts limiting their use of it to providing that service.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public.
  • If the business is acquired or merged — you'd be notified before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase — our database, authentication, and file storage provider. Your account data, activity data, and voice note audio are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In — optional, if you choose to sign in with Google instead of a password.
  • Your browser's push service (e.g. Apple, Google, or Mozilla's push infrastructure) — used to deliver notifications if you enable them.

6. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (or ask us to), we delete your profile, activity history, pipeline/CRM data, voice notes, and coach-relationship data within [X days], except where we're required to keep it longer by law. If you're a consultant, your coach's private notes about you are deleted at the same time.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access & export — you can export a backup of your own data at any time from the app's menu.
  • Correction — you can edit or delete almost everything you've entered directly in the App.
  • Deletion — contact us at privacy@captur.com.au to request full account deletion.
  • Unlinking a coach — a consultant can link to a different coach at any time; this does not retroactively delete history already shared with the previous coach.

If you're in the European Union / EEA

You have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, or erase your data, restrict or object to certain processing, and receive your data in a portable format. Our processing of your account and activity data relies on the performance of our contract with you (providing the App); pipeline/CRM data you enter about your own contacts is processed on the basis of your legitimate interests in running your business. You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority at any time.

If you're in Australia

We handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). You have the right to access and seek correction of the personal information we hold about you, and to make a complaint about how we've handled it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your data is restricted by row-level security policies scoped to your account and, where applicable, your linked coach or consultant — not by the app's interface alone. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

ELITE Tracker is intended for working professionals and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@captur.com.au
